# Courier modelling reference

Created with the built-in image generation tool using the original courier concept as the design reference.

Final image: ParcelPanicCourier_Modelling_Reference.png

The front, right-side and back views are intended as a visual modelling guide. The backpack, straps and parcel were omitted to expose the body and clothing. Main silhouette heights are aligned. The three views are an artist-style guide rather than dimensioned engineering drawings.

## Main prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: clean orthographic character turnaround reference sheet for learning to model this specific courier in Blender.
Input image 1: the original courier character design and identity reference; preserve this character's face, swept brown hair, backwards purple baseball cap, pink zip jacket over an off-white T-shirt, purple cargo trousers and navy sneakers. Use the ORIGINAL supplied character, not a different simplified mascot.
Primary request: Produce one wide landscape modelling sheet with THREE large, equally scaled, full-body orthographic views in a row: exact straight FRONT view on the left, exact RIGHT SIDE profile facing left in the centre, exact straight BACK view on the right. Same individual, same clothes, same anatomy, same scale and same neutral A-pose in every view. Front and back arms straight and about 25 degrees away from the torso, hands open relaxed with five clear fingers, palms directed towards thighs; legs straight, feet hip-width and pointing straight forward, weight even, no hip tilt. Head upright and looking straight forward. In the side view keep the identical pose and a precise 90 degree profile; do not make it three-quarter.
For this body modelling sheet REMOVE the backpack, shoulder straps and parcel in all three views so they do not obscure the body and jacket. Keep the cap, complete jacket, shirt, cargo trousers and shoes. Do not put hands in pockets. Keep the jacket construction and hairstyle consistent on front, side and back; cap brim points towards the character's BACK in every view. Same collar, cuffs, hems, pocket positions, sole thickness and anatomy heights. The whole head, cap, hands and shoes must be fully visible with generous clean margin.
Style: detailed smooth stylized 3D character reference like the supplied image, credible cartoon anatomy, friendly teenage courier, expressive brown eyes, actual defined facial planes and natural fabric folds, not a sketch and not low-poly. Prioritize clear forms and reference usability over dramatic art.
Layout: plain WHITE background, minimal evenly distributed neutral lighting, no cast shadows, no perspective, no camera tilt, no depth of field. Very faint thin grey horizontal alignment guides behind the figures at top of cap, chin, shoulders, waist, knees and soles, matching all views. All three soles share one horizontal baseline; all cap tops share one horizontal height. Simple small labels under each figure only: FRONT, RIGHT SIDE, BACK. No invented measurements, no extra diagrams, no other text, no watermark. Views must look like one 3D character rotated, not three redesigns.
